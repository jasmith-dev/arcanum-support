Arcanum privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Arcanum is a tool for writing and building visual novels. This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app.

We collect nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your stories, pictures, recordings or any other information to us or to anyone else. The app does not connect to the internet.

Where your work is kept. Everything you make is stored on your device, inside the app. The app also keeps its own daily backups there. If you use iCloud Backup for your device, Apple includes the app's data in that backup under its own terms. Deleting the app deletes your projects and its backups, so save a copy to the Files app first (File ▸ Back up).

Microphone. The app asks to use the microphone only if you choose to record a voice line. Recordings stay in your project on your device.

Files you share. When you export or publish a story, the file goes only where you send it. A story you publish can include features you switch on for your readers, such as sending a code to an address you choose; those go between your readers and you, never to us.

Children. The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version comes with an app update and carries a new date.

Contact. Help ▸ Report a problem in the app, or the support link on the App Store page.
